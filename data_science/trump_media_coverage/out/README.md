# Output files

## Important files
1. typology.md: a guide to how my team and I categorized each article.
2. /TF-IDF_by_category/bar_plots.ipynb: a visualisation of the top 10 words for each topic based on term frequency-inverse document frequency (TF-IDF) scores.

## TF-IDF
- TF: (number of times a given term appears in the csv for the category)/(number of terms in the category's csv)
- IDF: log((number of categories = 8)/(number of categories containing the term))
- The top scoring words in each category can be used to characterize the topic they belong to.
